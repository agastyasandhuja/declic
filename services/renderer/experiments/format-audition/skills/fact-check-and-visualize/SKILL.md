# Fact Check And Visualize

Extract claims from narration, labels and geometry. Link each to an approved source or an executable exact calculation. Verify units, conditions and intermediate states: free fall excludes air resistance, pendulums use small angles, ideal doubling omits limits, and shallow list copies may share nested objects. Distinguish schematic maps and hypothetical quantities from real data. Block a render for unsupported factual assertions; keep subjective art preferences separate. Research popularity claims are not evidence of learning.

These instructions are local candidate skills for the format audition. User review determines which formats to retain; they are not globally installed.
