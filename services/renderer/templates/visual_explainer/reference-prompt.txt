~100–115 words, no repeated conclusion. Open with small payment vs real total surprise.

not symbolic stock photos of calculators, money, students, offices or generic objects.
Image-led stories require >=4 distinct relevant assets, no adjacent reuse or more than
two uses. Compare actual evidence in those images. Never claim candidate images exist.
Sources and attribution go in metadata/credits, never permanent canvas text. Captions
carry narration. Keep only essential object labels and exact numbers on screen.

Caption and animation policy: use the shared white single-word speech-aligned
caption treatment. No yellow phrase captions. Follow ANIMATION_STANDARD.md: each
shot needs a physical start/action/end, task-facing subjects, contact continuity
and a camera that reveals the action. Reuse authored models/actions when suitable.
