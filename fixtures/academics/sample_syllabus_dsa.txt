SYNTHETIC COURSE SYLLABUS — for demo use only (not real course data)

Course: Introduction to Data Structures (DEMO 187)
Term: Fall 2026
Course time zone: America/New_York

Weekly units:
  Unit 1. Arrays and complexity — Big-O notation, array traversal.
  Unit 2. Linked lists — nodes and pointers, singly vs doubly linked,
          inserting and deleting nodes, iterative reversal of a singly linked list.
  Unit 3. Stacks and queues — LIFO/FIFO, array- and list-backed implementations.
  Unit 4. Trees — binary trees, traversals, binary search trees.

Assessments:
  Midterm 1 — Wednesday, October 14, 2026. Covers Units 1 and 2 (arrays and
    linked lists), including linked-list reversal. In-class.
  Final exam — cumulative. Date to be announced by the registrar.
  Weekly quizzes — most Fridays.

Textbook: "Introduction to Algorithms" by Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein,
  4th edition. Chapters assigned per unit.

Note: fast/slow pointer cycle detection is mentioned as optional enrichment and
is not required for any exam.
